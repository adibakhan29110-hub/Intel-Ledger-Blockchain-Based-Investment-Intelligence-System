# 🧠 Intel Ledger  
### Blockchain-Based Investment Intelligence System  

Intel Ledger is an AI + Blockchain-powered business intelligence platform designed for startups, investors, and stakeholders. The platform enables secure venture verification, AI-driven investment analysis, decentralized trust scoring, and blockchain-backed ownership of business assets and reports.

By combining Artificial Intelligence with decentralized technologies, Intel Ledger creates a transparent ecosystem where innovation, credibility, and investment intelligence coexist securely.
